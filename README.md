# Farmers Market Proposal
The  Farmers Market will be a platform designed to connect Minnesota’s local  farmers with  consumers using an online database to bring  a modern experience  to find their needs.The database  will include the farmer’s market location, vendors with a specific item and  they will be able to place online orders. We will as a group hope that we can show the following features:

### Support Local Farmers 
It makes it easier  for people to buy directly from small farmers, which helps local businesses thrive.

### Boost the Local Economy 
Vendors get more visibility and can sell more products by using a simple online system.

### Accessibility 
 Busy customers can quickly find markets, browse products, and pre-order items  without having to go to every
 market in person.

 ### 1.  Farmers Market Directory 

The farmers Markets can be  filtered by location, date, vendors, and specialty items.  If there are catalogs of the products and if Pre-ordering is available.

### 2. Admin Dashboard
  Can manage their inventory, view orders, and update market attendance for Vendors. 
              
### 3.  Payments actions 
Will be designed with payment options for online orders, show if vendors  accept only cash or cash/card.

### 4. Customer Experience 
Browse markets and vendors.

Search for products across all markets.

Place pre-orders with quantity and price details.

Select payment option (cash, card, or online transaction).

Receive order confirmation and pickup details.

### 5. Administration
Admins can manage farmers market listings, vendor accounts, and customer issues
ensure data consistency and system security.
